## Key Decisions & Architecture
- **State Machine Strictness**: Enforced transitions: QUEUED->PLANNING->DISCOVERING->RETRIEVING->EXTRACTING->VERIFYING->ANALYZING->SYNTHESIZING->COMPLETED.
- **Graceful Null Results**: Validated that runs with zero extracted evidence complete successfully as `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
- **Review Gate Isolation**: Deep runs with disputed claims pause in `REVIEW_REQUIRED` until human review decisions are posted.
- **Budget Ceilings**: Halted runaway runs exceeding `MAX_RUN_USD` and emitted `budget.warning` at 80% utilization.
