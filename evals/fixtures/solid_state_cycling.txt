# Solid-State Battery Cycling and Dendrite Suppression
Published: 2026-02-20
Domain: advanced-materials.org
Publisher: Wiley

Composite sulfide solid electrolytes have demonstrated continuous suppression of lithium dendrite propagation at 5 mA/cm2 current density.
Full-cell cycling demonstrated 85% capacity retention after 1000 continuous cycles at 45 degrees Celsius.
Stack pressure requirements were reduced to 1.5 MPa using compliant interlayers.
Manufacturing scale-up remains constrained by sensitivity to atmospheric moisture.
