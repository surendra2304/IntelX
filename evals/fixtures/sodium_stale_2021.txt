# Historical Overview: Early Sodium-Ion Chemistry
Published: 2021-01-10
Domain: archive-chem.org
Publisher: Academic Archive

Early commercial assessments of sodium-ion battery prototypes indicate strict physical limitations.
Prussian blue analogs currently achieve a maximum energy density of 90 Wh/kg, which is inadequate for electric vehicles.
Cycle life degrades rapidly beyond 300 cycles with substantial capacity fading.
Thermal runaway limits remain uncharacterized across wide temperature swings.
