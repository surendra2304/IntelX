# Laboratory Evaluation Report: Sodium-Ion Cathode Benchmarks
Published: 2026-03-15
Domain: nature-energy.org
Publisher: Nature Publishing Group

Experimental trials conducted at the Energy Storage Institute evaluated Prussian white and layered oxide cathodes.
The optimized layered oxide formulation demonstrated an active gravimetric energy density of 160 Wh/kg at 1C discharge rate.
Cyclical testing showed 92% capacity retention after 1200 full charge-discharge cycles under standard ambient temperatures.
The electrolyte exhibits significant thermal stability with no thermal runaway observed up to 180 degrees Celsius.
Commercial deployment in urban transport fleets is anticipated by late 2027.
