# High-Capacity Silicon Anode Energy Density Characterization
Published: 2026-05-01
Domain: nature.com
Publisher: Springer Nature

In this work, silicon-graphite composite anodes paired with nickel-rich cathodes demonstrated a gravimetric cell energy density of 420 Wh/kg.
The porous carbon matrix accommodated up to 280% volumetric expansion without mechanical pulverization.
Initial Coulombic efficiency reached 89.4% with full electrolyte compatibility.
